Privacy Policy — Claude Google MCP

Last updated: October 25, 2026

What this app is

Claude Google MCP is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It connects the Claude desktop app to the owner's own Google accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What data it accesses

With your permission, the app uses Google's APIs to access:

  • Gmail: reading, searching, labeling and drafting or sending email.
  • Google Calendar: reading, creating and updating events.
  • Google Drive: reading, searching and managing files.

How the data is used

Data is accessed only when you ask Claude to perform a task, such as searching your email or listing your events. The results are passed to the Claude AI assistant (provided by Anthropic) so it can answer your request. Anthropic's handling of that data is covered by Anthropic's privacy policy.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored only on your own computer, in a local configuration file. The app has no server of its own and does not keep copies of your Google data.

Sharing

Your Google data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone other than the AI assistant you are using to make the request.

Google API Services User Data Policy

This app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
